Myself | میرا تعارف

👦🏻👧🏻Children introducing themselves | بچے اپنا تعارف کرواتے ہوئے

English

My name is a special name that tells other people who I am. Every child has a name. I am a young student. I live with my family. I love my parents and teachers. I go to school to learn new things. I should speak politely, keep myself clean and help others.

Lesson 02

My Family | میرا خاندان

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦🏡A loving family | ایک پیارا خاندان

English

A family is a group of people who live together and care for one another. Our family gives us love, care and protection. A family may include a father, mother, brothers, sisters and grandparents. Parents teach us good habits. Grandparents share stories and experiences. We should respect every family member and help one another.

اردو

Lesson 03

My School | میرا اسکول

🏫📚🎒Our school | ہمارا اسکول

English

A school is a place where children learn. We go to school to read, write, count, play and discover new things. Our school has classrooms, teachers, books and a playground. Lesson 04

Parts of the Body | جسم کے حصے

🧒🏻👀👂👃👄✋Our body parts | ہمارے جسم کے حصے

English

Our body is made up of different parts. Every body part has an important job. We use our head to think. Our eyes help us see. Our ears help us hear. Our nose helps us smell. Our tongue helps us taste. Our hands help us hold things and our legs help us walk and run. We should take care of our body by eating healthy food, bathing and exercising.

اردو

Lesson 05

Five Senses | پانچ حواس

👁️👂👃👅🤚Five senses | پانچ حواس

English

We have five main senses. They help us understand the world around us. Eyes are used for seeing. Ears are used for hearing. The nose is used for smelling. The tongue is used for tasting. Skin helps us feel touch, such as soft, hard, hot or cold things. We should protect our sense organs and never put harmful things near them.

اردو

ہمارے پانچ اہم حواس ہیں۔ یہ ہمیں اپنے اردگرد کی دنیا کو سمجھنے میں مدد دیتے ہیں۔ آنکھوں سے ہم دیکھتے ہیں۔ کانوں سے سنتے ہیں۔ ناک سے خوشبو اور بدبو محسوس کرتے ہیں۔ زبان سے ذائقہ چکھتے ہیں۔ جلد سے ہم چھونے کا احساس کرتے ہیں، مثلاً نرم، سخت، گرم یا ٹھنڈی چیز۔ ہمیں اپنے حواس کے اعضا کی حفاظت کرنی چاہیے۔

Lesson 06

Colours | رنگ

🌈🎨🖍️Beautiful colours | خوبصورت رنگ

English

Colours make our world beautiful. We see many colours in flowers, fruits, clothes, toys and nature. Red is the colour of many apples. Green is the colour of grass. The sky often looks blue during a clear day. Yellow is the colour of a ripe banana. We can learn colours by looking carefully at things around us.

اردو

Shapes | اشکال

🔴 🟦 🔺 ⭐ ❤️Different shapes | مختلف اشکال

English

Shapes tell us about the form of objects. We see shapes everywhere. A ball is round like a circle. A book may look like a rectangle. A window may be square or rectangular. A slice of some foods may look like a triangle. Learning shapes helps us recognize and describe objects.

Lesson 08

Fruits | پھل

🍎🍌🍇🍊🍉🥭Fresh fruits | تازہ پھل

English

Fruits are an important part of healthy food. They contain vitamins, water and other nutrients that help our bodies stay healthy. Vegetables | سبزیاں

🥕🥔🍅🥒🌽🥬Fresh vegetables | تازہ سبزیاں

English

Vegetables are healthy foods that help our bodies grow and remain strong. We eat many kinds of vegetables. Carrots, potatoes, tomatoes, peas, spinach, cucumbers and onions are common vegetables. 🦁🐘🐄🐐🐈🐕Animals around us | ہمارے اردگرد جانور

Animals are living things. Some animals live with people, while others live in forests, deserts, rivers and oceans. A cow gives us milk. A horse can carry people. A lion is a wild animal. 🦜🕊️🦚🦆🐓🦉Beautiful birds | خوبصورت پرندے

English

Birds are animals with feathers, wings and beaks. Most birds have two legs. Many birds can fly, but some birds, such as ostriches and penguins, cannot fly. Lesson 24

Safety Rules | حفاظتی اصول

🚦🚸🛑⛑️Stay safe | محفوظ رہیں

English

Safety rules protect us from accidents. We should cross the road at a zebra crossing and look both ways before crossing. We should hold an adult's hand near busy roads. We must not play with matches, sharp tools or electrical sockets. We should never talk to or go anywhere with a stranger without permission from a trusted adult. If we need help, we should tell our parents or teachers.

اردو

Computer | کمپیوٹر

💻🖥️⌨️🖱️Learn about computers | کمپیوٹر کے بارے میں جانیں

English

A computer is an electronic machine. It helps us write, draw, learn and do many other tasks. A computer has different parts. The monitor shows pictures and words. The keyboard helps us type. The mouse helps us point and click. The CPU is an important processing part in many desktop computers. We should use computers carefully and with the guidance of an adult.

Lesson 26

Universe | کائنات

🌍🌙☀️⭐🪐🚀Our wonderful universe | ہماری حیرت انگیز کائنات

English

The universe is very vast. It contains stars, planets, moons and many other objects. The Sun is a star that gives us light and heat. Earth is the planet on which we live. The Moon is Earth's natural satellite. During the day we usually see the Sun, and at night we can often see the Moon and stars. We should learn about nature and take care of our planet.

اردو

Water | پانی

💧🌊🚰☔🐟Water is precious | پانی قیمتی ہے

English

Water is necessary for all living things. People, animals and plants need water to live. We use water for drinking, cooking, washing and cleaning. Rain fills rivers, lakes and dams. We should drink clean and safe water. We must not waste water by leaving taps open. Saving water helps protect our future.

Community Helpers | ہمارے مددگار

English

Community helpers are people who work to help others. Teachers educate children. Doctors and nurses care for sick people. Farmers grow crops and produce food. Sports and games are good for our health. They help us stay active, learn teamwork and follow rules. Children enjoy playing football, cricket, badminton and many other games.
